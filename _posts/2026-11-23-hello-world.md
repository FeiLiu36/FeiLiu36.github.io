Welcome to my blog. Most of my work shows up as papers, but papers leave out a lot: the half-formed ideas, the questions I keep coming back to, and the reasoning behind decisions that never makes it into a methods section.

This is a place for that. You'll find two kinds of writing here:

- **Essays**: longer pieces that work through a topic in some depth.
- **Thoughts**: short notes on a single idea, observation or question.

Every post has a comment section at the bottom. Questions, pushback and pointers to related work are all very welcome.
